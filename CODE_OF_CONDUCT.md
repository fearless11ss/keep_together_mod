# Code of conduct

Keep Together contributors and community members must communicate respectfully
and keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and project goals.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse,
doxxing, sexualized conduct directed at another participant, spam, malicious
code, impersonation, and publishing another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and restrict
participation when necessary to protect the project and its members. Reports
about conduct should use a private repository contact rather than a public
issue when they contain personal or sensitive information.

Enforcement decisions should record the behavior, evidence, action, duration,
and appeal outcome. Good-faith technical criticism and security research are
not violations merely because they are uncomfortable.
