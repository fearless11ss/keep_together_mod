# Public-launch legal gates

This checklist is not legal advice. Public beta remains blocked until qualified
Russian IT/telecom counsel records an answer for each gate.

## Minecraft and distribution

- [ ] Review the decision not to verify Microsoft ownership against the current
      Minecraft EULA and Usage Guidelines.
- [ ] Confirm that no Minecraft binary, modified client, launcher, Mojang asset,
      or official-looking branding is distributed.
- [ ] Publish the unofficial-product disclaimer everywhere the mod is offered.
- [ ] Review Modrinth and CurseForge disclosures before submission.

## Personal data

- [ ] Confirm the operator identity and legally required public contact/address.
- [ ] File and verify the Roskomnadzor processing notification.
- [ ] Confirm Russian location of PostgreSQL, object storage, logs, and backups.
- [ ] Map every purpose to data, legal basis, retention, processor, and location.
- [ ] Prepare separate legal acceptances and consent records where required.
- [ ] Confirm the treatment of authenticated Profile fields and consent to
      dissemination.
- [ ] Approve deletion, export, access, correction, and objection procedures.
- [ ] Approve the 24-hour/72-hour personal-data incident runbook.

## Messaging and cryptography

- [ ] Determine whether Keep Together is an organizer of information
      dissemination.
- [ ] Determine whether Direct and Group Conversations make it an instant
      messaging service.
- [ ] Approve the phone-identification procedure for Russian and foreign numbers.
- [ ] Reconcile mandatory retention with account deletion and history promises.
- [ ] Determine whether true zero-knowledge MLS E2E can lawfully launch.
- [ ] If it cannot, keep public Messaging disabled rather than adding an
      undisclosed decryption path.

## Relay

- [ ] Determine whether the public Relay is merely an application feature or a
      regulated communications service.
- [ ] Approve provider terms, traffic handling, logs, and lawful retention.
- [ ] Approve any foreign Relay region and associated cross-border transfer
      before use.

## Processors and transfers

- [ ] Approve hosting, object storage, backup, email, SMS, DNS/CDN, monitoring,
      and security processors.
- [ ] File and complete any required cross-border-transfer procedure before
      foreign processing begins.
- [ ] Publish processor categories, purposes, locations, and retention.

## Product documents

- [ ] Terms of service.
- [ ] Privacy policy.
- [ ] Acceptable-use and content policy.
- [ ] Age declaration.
- [ ] Copyright and abuse process.
- [ ] Security contact and vulnerability policy.
- [ ] Account deletion and export disclosures.

## Approval record

For each checked item, record the date, responsible reviewer, source documents,
approved design version, and any conditions. A verbal answer is not sufficient
for the public-beta gate.
